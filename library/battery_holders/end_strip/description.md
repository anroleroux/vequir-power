A strip that bolts across the end faces of single 18650 holders to join them:
long enough for three holders side by side, and wide enough to hold two rows
of them bottom to bottom. It has a countersunk hole for an M1.2 countersunk
screw at each of the holders' threaded holes, so the screw heads sit in the
strip.

Two tabs come out of the strip's bottom long side and turn 90 degrees, like bent
pieces but in plastic, as wide as the strip, in line with the two middle
fastener clusters. Each extends far enough to take one M1.2 countersunk
screw head, and has two next to each other, with the countersinks on the
inside of the bend.

Three material-saving holes go through the strip, between the fastener
clusters.

Little square pillars stick out of the strip the same way the tabs stick
out, 0.4 mm higher than the tabs, with just enough profile to take an M1.2
thread, and holes for the screws. They sit on the four corners of the
centre material-saving hole; the two near the tabs may touch them.
