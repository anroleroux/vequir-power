A clamping beam that sits between the end strip's pillars and the 3S BMS.
It screws into two of the pillars and stretches from one side of the BMS to
the other, so the board rests on two of them. Little clamps will go on its
ends later to hold the board's edges.

To clamp the board, the beam is extended past each edge of the board far
enough to take an M1.2 tap hole, and there it rises by the thickness of the
BMS's base board.
