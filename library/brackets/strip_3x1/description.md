A plain strip that bolts across the end faces of three single 18650 holders
standing side by side in one row, for a 3S (three in series) pack. It has a
countersunk hole for an M1.2 countersunk screw at each of the holders'
threaded holes, so all six screw heads sit in the strip. No pillars yet.

Two tabs come out of the strip's top long side and turn 90 degrees outward,
like bent pieces but in plastic, in line with the joints between the
holders. Each extends far enough to take one M1.2 countersunk screw head,
and has two next to each other. The countersinks are at the top of the tabs,
so the screws go in downward.
